Man v Man v Food — core-loop demo

▶ Play in your browser

Desktop browser, WebGL 2 required (Chrome, Edge, Firefox). First load downloads about 40 MB. Click the game to capture the mouse.

Controls

  • WASD move, Shift sprint, mouse look, F1 release mouse
  • E at a restaurant door: start a challenge. In the challenge, click food to eat; 1/2/3 Water / Soda / Tums; Esc give up
  • Left click punch. Break line of sight for 6 s to shake a hunter
  • Shop and Hospital are in the centre block
