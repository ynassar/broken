# Man v Man v Food

A 3D open-world food-challenge game. You're a YouTuber travelling the world to beat every restaurant food challenge, film it, grow the channel, and survive the restaurant owners who hunt you down for eating free.

Built with **Godot 4.3** (GDScript, GL Compatibility renderer). Every model, texture and character is generated by scripts in this repo; there are no downloaded assets.

## Current state: core-loop demo

One Los Angeles block with 6 real LA challenges, exercising the full loop:

1. Walk to a restaurant, press **E** at the door.
2. Optionally ask to film kitchen B-roll (RNG roll; success = +30% views, but the owner gets 150% health).
3. Eating minigame: click food on the plate; each bite has a pickup time and a chew time. The **fullness** bar fills faster the faster you eat. Pausing briefly lets it settle; pausing too long makes you bloat. Hit the top and keep eating and you vomit. Water / Soda / Tums (keys 1/2/3) modify it.
4. The video posts automatically: views, subscribers, ad revenue, viral roll, prize money or the meal bill.
5. If you won, the owner storms out and hunts you. Fight (left click) or break line of sight for 6 seconds to evade.
6. Dying costs a hospital bill and all non-protected items; you respawn at the hospital.
7. Progression Store sells utensils, consumables, weapons, camera gear and skills.

## Run it

- **Editor:** install [Godot 4.3](https://godotengine.org/download) (standard build), open `project.godot`, press F5.
- **CLI:** `godot --path .`
- **Exports:** `export_presets.cfg` has Linux, Windows and Web presets: `godot --headless --export-release Linux build/linux/mvmvf.x86_64` (needs export templates installed).

Controls: WASD move, Shift sprint, mouse look, E interact, left click punch, Esc give up (in a challenge), F1 toggle mouse capture.

## Tests and QA

```
godot --headless --path . tests/run.tscn                      # unit tests (eating sim, economy, game state)
xvfb-run -a godot --path . --rendering-driver opengl3 -- --qa  # scripted playthrough, screenshots into qa/
```
Add `--movie` after `--qa` together with `--write-movie qa/movie/f.png --fixed-fps 24` to record the playthrough.
